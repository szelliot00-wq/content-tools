# Scientists claim the universe is twice as old as believed

Source: https://geekspin.co/scientists-claim-the-universe-is-twice-as-old-as-believed/

## Summary
Researchers at the University of Ottawa propose that the universe is 26.7 billion years old — nearly twice the current standard estimate of ~13.8 billion years. Their model, called CCC+TL (Covarying Coupling Constants plus Tired Light), replaces dark matter and dark energy by positing that physical constants shift slowly over time and that light loses energy as it travels. The model has been tested against several major astronomical observations and published in the Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society, though it remains outside mainstream cosmology.

## Key takeaways
- The CCC+TL model estimates the universe at **26.7 billion years old**, vs. the standard ~13.8 billion years from the ΛCDM model.
- It eliminates the need for **dark matter and dark energy** by modifying gravity laws and cosmic expansion constants over time.
- The model correctly predicts the **Big Bang abundance of hydrogen and helium** and matches the historical expansion rate without dark energy.
- It explains **galactic rotation curves** in 175 galaxies — one of the strongest traditional arguments for dark matter.
- It offers a potential resolution to the **James Webb Space Telescope puzzle** of unexpectedly large early galaxies: an older universe simply gave them more time to form.
- The model is **not yet mainstream**; independent verification and further Webb observations are needed before broader acceptance.
