# Apple Music to open concert venue in Battersea Power Station

Source: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c607l2j2rj8ro

## Summary
Apple is opening a 600-capacity concert venue called Apple Music Hall beneath its European headquarters at Battersea Power Station in London. The intimate space will host live shows by both established and emerging artists, with all performances filmed and broadcast on Apple Music. The move is part of Apple's strategy to differentiate its streaming service from competitors by creating deeper connections between artists and fans, and comes amid a broader crisis in London's live music scene that saw 45 venues close in 2024 alone.

## Key takeaways
- Apple Music Hall will hold 600 people and is described as having more cameras and speakers than any venue of its size in the world.
- All shows will be ticketed but also live-streamed on Apple Music — the primary goal is content creation, not competing with the broader live music industry.
- The venue also houses new Apple Music radio studios, DJ mix rooms, writing camps, and rehearsal spaces, creating an all-in-one creative facility.
- Apple VP Oliver Schusser emphasized a focus on breaking new artists, citing concern that listeners are over-reliant on catalogue music.
- The project marks Apple's return to live music in London, nine years after it ended the iTunes Festival (2007–2014).
- The venue's design echoes the Art Deco architecture of Battersea Power Station, with acoustically engineered brickwork to control sound reflection.
- UK Culture Secretary Lisa Nandy welcomed the venue as a "huge vote of confidence" in Britain's creative sector.
