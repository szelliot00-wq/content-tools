# Pillowfort Is Closing

Source: https://www.pillowfort.social/posts/8413273

## Summary
Pillowfort, an independent social media platform, is shutting down on December 4th, 2026. Founder Julia announced the closure after being unable to find someone to take over leadership of a proposed non-profit transition. She cites a 2021 security incident as a turning point that killed the platform's momentum, combined with years of burnout from running a complex platform with a tiny part-time team.

## Key takeaways
- **Final date:** Pillowfort closes December 4th, 2026.
- **All subscriptions have been cancelled**, but paid features remain accessible until closing day.
- **Refunds are available** for purchases/donations made after August 5th, 2026 — contact Staff to request one.
- **A blog export tool is coming** to save posts as a text file; the Tassel browser extension can also archive your blog in the meantime.
- The **2021 security incident** (which took the site down for weeks) is identified as the key inflection point from which the platform never recovered.
- The team was always very small — at peak, one community manager, one content moderator, and three part-time developers.
- The founder expressed hope that **someone else will build a better independent platform**, noting the need has only grown since Pillowfort launched nearly a decade ago.
