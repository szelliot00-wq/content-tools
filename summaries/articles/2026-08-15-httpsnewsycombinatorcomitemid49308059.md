# Ask HN: How do you keep up with HN these days?

Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49308059

## Summary
An HN user asked how others cope with the increasing volume of content on Hacker News, attributing the difficulty largely to the surge of AI-related posts over the past 2-3 years. The thread drew 32 responses ranging from "just accept missing things" to concrete tool recommendations. Sentiment was mixed — some users are adapting with filters and clients, others are burning out on the platform entirely.

## Key takeaways
- **Accept imperfection**: Several users emphasized letting go of FOMO — if something is truly important, it'll resurface elsewhere.
- **RSS with point thresholds**: A popular approach is using `hnrss.org/newest?points=100` to only see posts that have cleared 100 upvotes, cutting noise dramatically (117 posts → 29 in one user's comparison).
- **Third-party clients help**: Tools like hckrnews.com, hn.leftium.com ("Seeking Orange"), and hckrnight.com offer better filtering, highlighting, and view options than the default site.
- **"Past" and "Best" pages**: Using HN's built-in "past" link or `news.ycombinator.com/best` to skim top posts from prior days is a lightweight catch-up method.
- **AI-powered bots**: At least one user built a bot that monitors the HN top 10, summarizes posts with AI, and pushes notifications — a more automated approach to curation.
- **Weekly newsletter**: `hackernewsletter.com` surfaces the week's most important links for low-frequency readers.
- **Filter AI content manually**: Some users just skip all MCP, "agent," and frontier-lab posts and read whatever's left.
- **Platform fatigue is real**: Multiple users noted HN feels more toxic or lower quality than before, with one recommending the `noprocrast` setting to limit visits.
- **Follow quality users**: One user's strategy is to track individual HN accounts known for high-quality posts rather than scanning the firehose.
