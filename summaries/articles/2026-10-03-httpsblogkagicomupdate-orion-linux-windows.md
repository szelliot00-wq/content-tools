# An Update on Orion for Linux and Windows

Source: https://blog.kagi.com/update-orion-linux-windows

## Summary
Kagi announced on October 2, 2026 that it is discontinuing direct development of Orion browser for Linux and Windows. The decision stems from the reality of being a small, independently funded team that cannot sustainably build and maintain a cross-platform browser. Instead, Kagi will open-source the Linux and Windows work and refocus entirely on Orion for macOS and iOS.

## Key takeaways
- Kagi is halting development of Orion for Linux and Windows to concentrate resources on Apple platforms.
- The Linux/Windows Orion code will be open-sourced, with Kagi reaching out to open-source foundations for long-term stewardship — Kagi itself will not be the core maintainer.
- The team is small and entirely user-funded, making multi-platform browser development unsustainable without venture capital or a large open-source community.
- Orion for macOS and iOS will continue and is expected to improve faster now that the full team is focused on it.
- Kagi expressed appreciation for early Linux testers and bug reporters, framing the open-sourcing as giving their work back to the community.
