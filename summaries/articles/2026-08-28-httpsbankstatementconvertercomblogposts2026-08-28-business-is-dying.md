# My Business Is Dying

Source: https://bankstatementconverter.com/blog/posts/2026-08-28-business-is-dying/

## Summary
The author of Bank Statement Converter is reporting a significant business decline since peaking in February 2026, with revenue down 24% and Monthly Recurring Revenue down 12%. The core problem is a sharp drop in new subscriber acquisition (from 191/month in January to 45 in August) combined with rising cancellations, resulting in consistently negative MRR growth from April onward. The author draws a parallel to a previous app, Girlfriend Plus, which followed a similar trajectory of revenue spike followed by neglect and eventual irrelevance.

## Key takeaways
- Revenue is down 24% and MRR is down 12% from the February 2026 peak.
- MRR growth turned sharply negative starting in April 2026, with July being the worst month at -$19,960 HKD.
- New subscriber counts have fallen nearly 75% — from 191 in January to just 45 in August.
- The author suspects the dual problem of fewer new subscribers and more cancellations driving the decline.
- Planned responses include analyzing cancellation reasons, doing marketing, and talking to users directly.
- The author is also considering getting a job as a fallback, signaling serious concern about the business's viability.
- This mirrors a past failure (Girlfriend Plus, 2017), suggesting a pattern of not sustaining development momentum after an initial revenue spike.
