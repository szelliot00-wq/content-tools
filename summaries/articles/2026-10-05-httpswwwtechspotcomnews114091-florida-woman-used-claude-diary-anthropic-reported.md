# Anthropic reported diary entry to police, woman faces felony charge

Source: https://www.techspot.com/news/114091-florida-woman-used-claude-diary-anthropic-reported-shoot.html

## Summary
A Florida woman, Carli Michelle Heller, is facing a second-degree felony charge after writing in Claude what she described as diary entries, including a statement about planning to "shoot up" a Sheriff's office. Claude's safety systems flagged the entry, a human reviewer deemed it a credible threat, and Anthropic reported it to law enforcement. Deputies visited her home and she was detained; she now faces charges under Florida Statute 836.10.

## Key takeaways
- AI chatbots are not private diaries — conversations can be reviewed by human contractors and reported to authorities.
- Anthropic's policy allows sharing user data in emergencies where disclosure may prevent death or serious physical injury.
- Florida Statute 836.10 makes it a second-degree felony to transmit a written or electronic threat of violence, mass shooting, or terrorism.
- This incident follows broader legal pressure on AI companies, including lawsuits against OpenAI related to mass shootings where prior warning signs existed in user conversations.
- The case highlights a key distinction between Anthropic and OpenAI's response thresholds: Anthropic escalated and reported, while OpenAI previously did not in a comparable situation.
