# Homeland Security spied on Minnesotans who spoke out against ICE

Source: https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/08/13/attorney-says-homeland-security-spied-on-minnesotans-who-opposed-ice

## Summary
A defense attorney for one of 15 Minnesotans charged with conspiracy to impede federal officers has filed a federal court motion alleging that Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) ran a covert surveillance program — dubbed "Operation Puppet Master" — targeting people who publicly opposed ICE enforcement in Minnesota. Undercover agents reportedly attended public meetings at churches, libraries, parks, and union halls, secretly recording conversations and building dossiers on ordinary citizens exercising First Amendment rights. The motion also alleges HSI obtained financial records from unions and misrepresented them to grand jurors as evidence of conspiracy.

## Key takeaways
- HSI launched "Operation Puppet Master" just four days after Alex Pretti was shot and killed by federal agents, framing it as targeting "violent opportunists."
- Undercover agents infiltrated public meetings at churches, parks, libraries, and union halls, recording conversations and creating agency-wide dossiers on attendees.
- Financial records from unions SEIU and Communications Workers of America were obtained and allegedly presented to grand jurors as support for the conspiracy — without, the attorney argues, legal justification.
- A second program, "Operation Whipple Shield," appears to have surveilled protesters at the federal building throughout the winter.
- Defense attorney Kevin Riach argues the surveillance was politically motivated — targeting people the Trump administration views as enemies — not a legitimate law enforcement effort.
- The motion seeks full disclosure of surveillance records and any communications showing officials were targeting protected First Amendment speech.
- All 15 defendants have pleaded not guilty; the U.S. attorney has maintained they were charged for actions, not speech.
