# EU now fears US interference in European elections

Source: https://www.eunews.it/en/2026/07/29/eu-now-fears-us-interference-in-european-elections/

## Summary
The EU is growing concerned about potential US interference in European elections, a worry raised in the European Parliament by non-attached MEP Fabio De Masi. The European Commission, responding through Justice Commissioner Michael McGrath, did not explicitly rule out the US as a threat, implicitly placing Trump's America alongside Russia and China as sources of foreign interference risk. The Commission says it is working with member states to protect electoral integrity, monitor foreign funding, and strengthen cooperation mechanisms.

## Key takeaways
- MEP Fabio De Masi formally asked the European Commission whether it is prepared to counter US electoral interference, signaling this concern has reached the institutional level.
- The Commission's response notably did not exclude the US as a potential threat, a significant diplomatic signal given the US is a traditional ally.
- Elon Musk's open support for Germany's far-right AfD party and J.D. Vance's endorsement of Viktor Orbán are cited as concrete examples of US-linked figures meddling in EU member state elections.
- The Commission is investigating potentially harmful political funding flows originating from the US through its European Cooperation Network on Elections.
- Several high-stakes EU elections are approaching (Germany, France, and others), raising the urgency of addressing foreign interference before those votes occur.
- Electoral conduct formally remains the competence of member states, limiting how directly the Commission can act, though it pledges cooperation and coordination.
