# OpenClaw 2.0, Accidentally

Source: https://openclaw.ai/blog/openclaw-2-accidentally

## Summary
OpenClaw 2.0 is the largest update in the project's history, built by 933 contributors across 16,000+ pull requests over nearly two months. What began as an effort to simplify installation and rebuild the browser app as a first-class experience gradually expanded into a full overhaul touching every part of the platform. The release also introduces multiplayer shared sessions, enabling teams and families to collaborate on agent workflows with full context preserved.

## Key takeaways
- The release comprises roughly 50% of all pull requests ever merged into OpenClaw, making it a foundational milestone rather than an incremental update.
- Installation was overhauled to detect existing subscriptions (ChatGPT, Claude) and local models, reducing setup friction so users reach a working conversation faster.
- The browser app was fully rebuilt as a primary interface, not an afterthought, allowing users to continue setup, return to work, and observe live agent activity.
- Shared cloud sessions turn OpenClaw into a multiplayer tool, letting teams hand off work or collaborate with full context intact.
- The platform is designed to grow incrementally — from a simple single-inbox workflow to complex, multi-service automations — without increasing operational complexity for the user.
- OpenClaw is open source and explicitly positions itself against vendor lock-in, supporting multiple AI providers and models.
