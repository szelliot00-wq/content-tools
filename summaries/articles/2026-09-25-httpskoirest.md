# Show HN: Koi.rest – watch some fish and regain your balance

Source: https://koi.rest

## Summary
Koi.rest is a web-based relaxation tool built by Paul Glushak (hxii) that displays an animated koi fish scene accompanied by layered ambient sounds. Users can customize a soundscape of wind, birds, chimes, crickets, stream, and rain, each with individual volume controls. The app also includes a built-in countdown timer (10, 15, 30, or 60 minutes) to structure a short mental break.

## Key takeaways
- A minimal browser-based relaxation/focus tool centered on a koi fish visual and ambient audio.
- Offers six independently toggleable and volume-adjustable sound layers: wind, birds, chimes, crickets, stream, and rain.
- Built-in timer presets (10, 15, 30, 60 minutes) plus a custom option let users set a defined break duration.
- Designed for quick mental resets — the landing page prompt "enter" and tagline "regain your balance" signal its intended use case.
- Created by solo developer Paul Glushak (hxii).
