# Extra Big Ass Intelligence

Source: https://www.extrabigassintelligence.com/

## Summary
This is not an article — it's the UI structure of a satirical webpage called "Extra Big Ass Intelligence," a parody of AI hype culture inspired by the film *Idiocracy*. It spoofs themes like government AI rebranding (banning the word "AI" by executive order), absurd advertising, surge pricing, and mindless chatbot prompts. The site is a comedic critique of tech industry excess, political spectacle, and consumer manipulation.

## Key takeaways
- The site parodies AI rebranding: "Artificial Intelligence" is fictionally outlawed and replaced with "Super Intelligence (SI)" by executive decree.
- It satirizes AI hype through *Idiocracy* references, including Brawndo ("It's got electrolytes") as a sponsor and "Ow! My Balls!" as live TV programming.
- Fake ads mock real trends: local AI models marketed with "zero refusal vectors," dopamine injections for depressed AI, and dynamic burger pricing based on pupil dilation and credit scores.
- Pre-loaded chat prompts ("Show me your tits!", "Why will Super Intelligence save the economy?") mock the mundane and often inappropriate ways people actually use AI chatbots.
- The running counters ("Teraflops wasted today", "Lifetimes of regret accumulated") underscore the site's thesis: AI consumption is wasteful and culturally corrosive.
