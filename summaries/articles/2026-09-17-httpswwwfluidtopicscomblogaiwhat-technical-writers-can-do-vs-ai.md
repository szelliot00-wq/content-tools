# What Can Tech Writers Do in the Age Of AI?

Source: https://www.fluidtopics.com/blog/ai/what-technical-writers-can-do-vs-ai/

## Summary
This article is the second in a series exploring the evolving role of technical writers alongside AI. It identifies content workflows where AI assistance is well-suited, while also highlighting the human skills that remain irreplaceable. The piece argues that technical writers still have a meaningful place in the industry, but their role will shift toward oversight, strategy, and tasks requiring human judgment.

## Key takeaways
- AI will not replace technical writers, but it will change what they spend their time on.
- Certain content workflows — likely repetitive, high-volume, or structured tasks — are good candidates for AI management.
- Technical writers possess irreplaceable human skills that AI cannot replicate, such as contextual judgment, audience empathy, and subject-matter collaboration.
- Some tasks must remain under human control to ensure accuracy, tone, and quality.
- Writers who adapt by learning to work alongside AI tools will be better positioned than those who resist the change.
