# Suits Are Better Tech Than Modern Clothes

Source: https://devz.cl/posts/the-lost-tech-in-contemporary-clothing/

## Summary
The author argues that suits are superior to modern casual clothing on two fronts: comfort and utility. After having suits tailored to their non-standard body proportions, they found formal wear fits far better than off-the-rack modern clothes. The article also makes a case that suits function like "original techwear" — offering more pockets, more durable fasteners, and better longevity than contemporary fashion.

## Key takeaways
- Tailoring makes suits adaptable to individual body proportions, while modern clothes use fixed sizing that doesn't account for variation — a major comfort disadvantage for many people.
- Suits offer significantly more pocket space (three front pockets, two inner pockets, a pencil slot) than typical modern clothing, reducing or eliminating the need for a backpack or bag.
- Buttons are more repairable than zippers; a fallen button is easy to replace, while a broken zipper typically requires full replacement.
- Modern clothing tends to wear out faster — the author notes t-shirts developing holes within a few years, while a traditional coat gifted by their father has lasted decades.
- Women's clothing is particularly poor on functionality, with pockets so small they're effectively useless, forcing reliance on handbags.
- Despite preferring suits functionally and physically, the author concedes social norms make wearing them in casual settings awkward — highlighting that fashion conventions override practicality in everyday life.
