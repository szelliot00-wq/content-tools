# Show HN: Old Nvidia and ATI tech demos, running again in the browser

Source: https://gpu-demo-gallery.pages.dev/

## Summary
This is a showcase of classic GPU tech demos from 1998–2006, originally created by NVIDIA, 3dfx, and ATI to demonstrate the capabilities of their graphics hardware, now ported to run in the browser. The collection spans landmark GPU generations from the 3dfx Voodoo2 through the NVIDIA GeForce 8, covering effects like bump mapping, reflections, fur rendering, and fluid simulation.

## Key takeaways
- 22 historical GPU demos have been preserved and made playable in the browser, covering nearly a decade of graphics hardware history (1998–2006).
- The demos represent milestone GPUs: 3dfx Voodoo2, NVIDIA GeForce 256/2/3/4 Ti/8, and ATI Radeon 9700.
- Each demo was designed to showcase a specific rendering technique of its era — multi-pass texturing, bump mapping, caustics, procedural grass, fur shading, non-photorealistic rendering, and GPU-side fluid simulation.
- Both major GPU rivals of the early 2000s are represented: NVIDIA demos dominate the earlier years, while ATI's Radeon 9700 entries (2002–2003) demonstrate that card's shader capabilities.
- The demos are interactive, not just videos — users can poke, click, stir, pilot, and manipulate the scenes.
