# Kusama Yayoi Dies at 97

Source: https://news.jp/i/1465528042000662661

## Summary
Japanese avant-garde artist Yayoi Kusama died on the 14th at a Tokyo hospital from multiple organ failure, aged 97. Born in Nagano Prefecture, she was internationally celebrated for her colorful polka-dot and net-pattern works, as well as her iconic pumpkin sculptures. She spent much of her career based in New York from 1957 to 1973, and received the Order of Culture from the Japanese government.

## Key takeaways
- Kusama died at 97 from multiple organ failure; a private funeral has been held with a public memorial planned for a later date.
- She moved to New York in 1957, becoming known as the "Queen of Happenings" for anti-Vietnam War street performances featuring nude body painting.
- Her signature polka-dot and net motifs originated from hallucinations she experienced from childhood, which she used art to confront and process.
- Her work spanned painting, printmaking, soft sculpture, and large-scale installations, including the famous *Pumpkin* sculpture on Naoshima Island in Kagawa Prefecture.
- International recognition surged in the 1990s onward, highlighted by representing Japan at the 1993 Venice Biennale, a 1998 solo show at MoMA, and major retrospectives across Europe and the US in 2011–12.
