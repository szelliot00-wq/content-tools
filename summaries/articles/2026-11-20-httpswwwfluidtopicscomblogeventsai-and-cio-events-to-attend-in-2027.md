# Top AI and CIO Events to Attend in 2027

Source: https://www.fluidtopics.com/blog/events/ai-and-cio-events-to-attend-in-2027/

## Summary
Published November 18, 2026 by Kelly Dell, this article from Fluid Topics lists 22 AI and CIO conferences scheduled throughout 2027. It covers events across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and beyond, spanning topics like cybersecurity, AI adoption, cloud infrastructure, digital transformation, and enterprise architecture. The guide is intended to help IT leaders and CIOs plan their year by comparing dates, locations, and focus areas.

## Key takeaways
- **22 events are listed** spanning February through December 2027, covering a wide geographic range including the US, UK, Europe, Australia, and Latin America.
- **Major flagship events** include Google Cloud Next (Apr 13–15, Las Vegas), AWS re:Invent (Nov 29–Dec 3, Las Vegas), Web Summit (Nov 8–11, Lisbon), and MIT Sloan CIO Symposium (May 18, Cambridge).
- **AI is the dominant theme** across nearly all events, with a focus on moving AI from pilot to production, agentic AI, generative AI, and AI governance.
- **Cybersecurity is a close second theme**, featured prominently at Swiss Cyber AI, CSO Cybersecurity Awards, FranSec, it-sa Expo, and the American CIO & Cybersecurity Summit.
- **Several events are invitation-only** or executive-focused (CIO Edge, CIO & Chief AI Officer Exchange), targeting senior IT and AI leadership specifically.
- **Multi-city touring series** include Digital Enterprise CIO Transformation Assembly (9 cities), TechEx (3 cities), apidays (7+ cities), and Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo (2 locations).
- **The article is a living document** — dates and agendas are still being announced, and the post will be updated as organizers confirm 2027 details.
