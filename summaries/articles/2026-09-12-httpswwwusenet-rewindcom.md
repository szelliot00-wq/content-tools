# Usenet rewind archive search engine

Source: https://www.usenet-rewind.com/

## Summary
Usenet-Rewind is a searchable archive of Usenet newsgroup posts spanning from 1981 to the present. It provides access to over a billion text messages covering early internet discussions on topics like tech support, science, hobbyist communities, and news commentary. The service is operated by Erie Data Systems, LLC and offers both basic and advanced search, along with browsing by newsgroup or decade.

## Key takeaways
- The archive covers 45 years of Usenet history (1981–2026), with over 1 billion total messages indexed.
- Message volume grew explosively through the 1990s, peaking around 2004 (~104 million messages) before declining steadily as social media rose.
- Usenet activity never fully died — millions of messages per year are still being posted as recently as 2026.
- The site is positioned as a research tool for internet history, preserving firsthand accounts of online culture before the modern web.
- Features include full-text search, advanced search, newsgroup browsing, and topic/people/events browsing by decade.
