# AI is killing All About Berlin

Source: https://nicolasbouliane.com/blog/death-by-ai

## Summary
Nicolas Bouliane, who has run All About Berlin since 2017 and relied on it as his primary income since 2020, has seen the site lose 75% of its traffic after Google introduced AI Overviews, which surface AI-generated answers trained on his content before linking to his site. Beyond the financial hit, he describes losing the personal connection with his audience and the sense of purpose that came from creating genuinely useful work. He sees this as part of a broader trend affecting independent web publishers, with Google announcing plans to replace search results entirely with AI-generated, ad-supported answers.

## Key takeaways
- Google AI Overviews have cut All About Berlin's traffic by 75%, threatening the livelihood of a creator who has depended on it full-time since 2020.
- The model lets Google profit from content (via ads in AI answers) while the original creator loses both traffic and direct audience connection.
- This is not isolated — many independent site owners are paywalling, abandoning updates, or shutting down in response to the same dynamic.
- Google has announced AI-generated answers will fully replace traditional search results and will include ads, pointing toward a web with fewer destination sites.
- The human value of independent publishing — community, trust, personal voice — erodes when content is dissolved into anonymous AI-generated summaries.
