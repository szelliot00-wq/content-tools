# Framework discloses data breach via Metabase 0-day

Source: https://community.frame.work/t/framework-data-breach-discussion/83939

## Summary
Framework Computer disclosed a data breach affecting customer information (excluding billing/payment data), which originated from a zero-day vulnerability in Metabase, a third-party analytics tool. Community members praised Framework's response as unusually fast and transparent: Metabase notified business partners within 3 days of discovering the incident, and Framework confirmed and notified customers shortly after receiving that notice.

## Key takeaways
- The breach exposed some customer information but no payment data; Framework uses Stripe for payments, which was not affected.
- Metabase, the analytics platform used by Framework, had a zero-day vulnerability that was the root cause of the breach.
- Metabase notified affected business partners within 3 days of discovering the incident — considered a fast turnaround for security disclosure.
- Framework notified customers very quickly after receiving Metabase's notice, which community members called exceptional compared to industry norms.
- Community sentiment was largely positive toward Framework's handling, with users highlighting speed and transparency as the right model for incident response.
- This is not Framework's first disclosed breach; related topics reference prior incidents in January 2024 and June 2025.
