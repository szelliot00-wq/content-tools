# We Won't "Just Hang Out"

Source: https://borretti.me/article/we-wont-just-hang-out

## Summary
The article argues that the post-Singularity "infinite vacation" utopia won't work because the social infrastructure that enables meaningful human connection depends on shared work, education, and communities of practice — all of which AI renders obsolete. Without reasons to gather, collaborate, or develop skills, people will have neither the means nor the motivation to form deep relationships, leaving future generations unable to build the social networks that give life meaning.

## Key takeaways
- Work, education, and communities of practice are the primary mechanisms by which people meet and build lasting friendships — AI makes all of these superfluous.
- AI disintermediates human-to-human interaction: where someone once turned to an IRC community for help (and made friends), they now just ask an AI.
- The "best alternative to hanging out" (AI-generated entertainment, superstimuli, TikTok) keeps improving, reducing the motivation for people to seek real social connection.
- People in an AI utopia will lack skills, accomplishments, and education, making them less interesting as social companions — undermining the desire to hang out even if the opportunity exists.
- Even for people with pre-Singularity friendships, social networks may be non-renewable: relationships attrite over time and cannot be replenished through the same community-building processes that no longer exist.
- Cities lose their purpose without work, removing the geographic concentration that facilitates spontaneous social connection.
