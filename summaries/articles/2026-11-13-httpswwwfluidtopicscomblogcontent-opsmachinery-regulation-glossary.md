## Summary
This article from Fluid Topics provides a glossary of key terms defined in EU Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230. It targets technical documentation teams who need to understand precise regulatory language to achieve and maintain compliance. The article covers terms ranging from intended use and residual risk to data carrier and expected lifetime.

## Key takeaways
- EU Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 introduces specific defined terms that technical documentation teams must understand for compliance.
- Key concepts include "intended use," "residual risk," "data carrier," and "expected lifetime," each with regulatory significance.
- Precise understanding of these definitions is essential for producing accurate and compliant technical documentation.
- Documentation teams should treat the regulation's glossary as a foundational reference when preparing machinery compliance materials.
