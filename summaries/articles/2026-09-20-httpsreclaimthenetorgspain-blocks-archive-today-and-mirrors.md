# Spain Orders Blocks on Archive.today and Its Mirrors

Source: https://reclaimthenet.org/spain-blocks-archive-today-and-mirrors

## Summary
Spain's Intellectual Property Commission has ordered internet service providers to block Archive.today and its mirror domains (including Archive.is). The block was triggered by an administrative decision — not a court ruling — following a complaint from an unnamed rights holder. The official justification is that Archive.today "illegally facilitates access to content protected by intellectual property rights."

## Key takeaways
- Spain's Second Section of the Intellectual Property Commission issued the block order, making it an administrative rather than judicial action.
- The complainant is an unnamed rights holder, raising transparency concerns about who is driving the censorship.
- The block covers Archive.today and all its mirror domains, making circumvention harder.
- The stated reason is copyright infringement — specifically, facilitating illegal access to protected content — though archive services are broadly used for legitimate purposes like preserving public records and news articles.
- This follows a pattern of Spain targeting archiving and streaming-adjacent sites, as the same commission has previously gone after sites carrying sports broadcasts.
