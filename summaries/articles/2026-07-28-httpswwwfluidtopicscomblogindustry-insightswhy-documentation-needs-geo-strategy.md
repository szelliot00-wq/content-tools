# Why Your Documentation Needs a GEO Strategy Now

Source: https://www.fluidtopics.com/blog/industry-insights/why-documentation-needs-geo-strategy/

## Summary
Users are shifting away from reading product documentation directly, instead querying AI-powered answer engines for product information. This creates a risk that AI systems may surface inaccurate or outdated information from third-party sources rather than authoritative documentation. The article argues that documentation teams need to adopt a Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) strategy to ensure their content is accurately represented by AI systems.

## Key takeaways
- AI answer engines are becoming a primary channel through which users discover and consume product information.
- Documentation teams can no longer assume users will read source documentation directly — AI intermediaries now filter and repackage that content.
- Inaccurate third-party information surfaced by AI engines poses a real risk to customer experience and product trust.
- GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is an emerging discipline for structuring and publishing documentation so AI systems retrieve and represent it accurately.
- Acting on a GEO strategy now is framed as urgent, implying the window for early adoption advantage is open but closing.
