# Why Fibonacci?

Source: https://www.evilgeniuslabs.org/why-fibonacci

## Summary
"Why Fibonacci?" by Evil Genius Labs (posted November 16, 2026) explains the mathematical principles behind a series of Fibonacci-patterned LED boards. The author traces their inspiration back to a 2015 video by Jim Bumgardner, and describes how every LED board they design — from the tiny Fibonacci64 to the Fibonacci1024 — places LEDs on a Fermat spiral, mirroring the seed pattern of a sunflower. The article walks through the construction rules step by step, covering the golden angle (137.508°) and √n radial spacing as the two key mathematical ingredients.

## Key takeaways
- Every LED on a Fibonacci board is placed using two rules: rotate 137.508° (the golden angle) from the previous LED, and move √n distance from the center, where n is the LED's index.
- The pattern used is a **Fermat spiral** — the same geometry as sunflower seeds — not the commonly depicted "Fibonacci spiral" drawn from squares.
- The golden angle and √n spacing together produce the characteristic phyllotaxis pattern seen throughout nature (sunflowers, pinecones, etc.).
- The design scales across board sizes, from the one-inch Fibonacci64 all the way up to the Fibonacci1024.
- The author credits Jim Bumgardner's 2015 video as the original inspiration for the entire Fibonacci LED project series.
