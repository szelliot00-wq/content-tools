# JetZero

Source: https://www.jetzero.aero

## Summary
JetZero is an aerospace company developing the Z4, a blended-wing-body commercial jetliner designed to carry 250 passengers on international routes with significantly improved fuel efficiency. The company has attracted major investment, including a $175M Series B round and a strategic investment from Alaska Airlines. JetZero emphasizes aerodynamic innovation, passenger experience, and a long-term path toward zero-emission aviation.

## Key takeaways
- The Z4 jetliner uses up to 50% less fuel than conventional commercial aircraft due to its all-wing, low-drag design.
- JetZero raised $175M in a Series B funding round, signaling strong investor confidence.
- Alaska Airlines has made a strategic investment in JetZero, lending airline-industry validation to the concept.
- The company is targeting 250 seats and international range — positioning the Z4 as a direct competitor to mainline narrowbody and widebody jets.
- JetZero's roadmap includes a "clear path to zero" emissions, suggesting compatibility with sustainable aviation fuels or future propulsion technologies.
- The team touts deep industry experience as a core differentiator.
- The company is headquartered in Long Beach, California and is actively hiring.
