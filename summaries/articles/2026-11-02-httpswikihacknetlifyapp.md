# Show HN: Wikihack – HackerTube, but for Wikipedia articles mentioned on HN

Source: https://wikihack.netlify.app

## Summary
WikiHack is a web project shared on Hacker News that surfaces Wikipedia articles linked or mentioned in HN discussions, presented in a video-feed style (like YouTube/HackerTube but for Wikipedia). The site offers browsing modes including Top, Rising, Front Page, and Random, along with topic filtering and search. The content provided is a UI/navigation snapshot rather than a full article, so detail is limited.

## Key takeaways
- WikiHack aggregates Wikipedia articles that appear in Hacker News threads, making them discoverable in a feed format.
- Browsing modes (Top, Rising, Front Page, Random) mirror social news site conventions for surfacing popular or trending content.
- A search box and topic filters allow targeted exploration beyond the algorithmically ranked feeds.
- The project is a "Show HN" submission, meaning it was built and shared by an independent developer for community feedback.
