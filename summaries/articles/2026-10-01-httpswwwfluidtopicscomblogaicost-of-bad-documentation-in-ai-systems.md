# The Real Cost of Bad Documentation in AI Systems

Source: https://www.fluidtopics.com/blog/ai/cost-of-bad-documentation-in-ai-systems/

## Summary
Companies are increasing AI investments but seeing stagnant ROI, and the root cause is often misdiagnosed. The real problem is not the AI technology itself, but the quality and accessibility of the knowledge and documentation that powers these systems. Poor documentation directly impacts AI reliability and drives up costs.

## Key takeaways
- AI ROI problems are frequently caused by bad documentation, not flawed technology.
- The quality and accessibility of knowledge powering AI systems is a critical, often overlooked factor.
- Bad documentation increases costs and reduces the reliability of AI outputs.
- Fixing documentation quality is a lever for improving AI system performance and business outcomes.
