# Linear Algebra Done Right – Sheldon Axler

Source: https://linear.axler.net/

## Summary
*Linear Algebra Done Right* by Sheldon Axler is a best-selling undergraduate and graduate textbook for a second course in linear algebra, now in its fourth edition. The book takes a distinctive approach by deferring determinants to the end, instead centering the treatment on the structure of linear operators on finite-dimensional vector spaces. It is available in multiple formats and languages, and has been adopted by over 430 universities and colleges worldwide.

## Key takeaways
- The book targets undergraduate math majors and graduate students as a second course in linear algebra.
- Its defining pedagogical choice is to banish determinants to the end, keeping focus on linear operators and vector space structure.
- The fourth edition is available as a free PDF (English and Chinese), Kindle, and print; a third edition exists in Basque and Chinese print.
- The author prioritizes motivation of concepts and simplified proofs, supported by exercises in each chapter.
- The book has been widely adopted — used at 430+ institutions — and is accompanied by supplementary videos and errata.
