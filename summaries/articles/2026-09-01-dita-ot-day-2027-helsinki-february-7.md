# DITA-OT Day 2027 — Helsinki, February 7

Source: 

## Summary
DITA-OT Day 2027 is the 11th edition of the annual conference dedicated to the DITA Open Toolkit project, scheduled for February 7, 2027, in Helsinki, Finland. It is a free, in-person event held at the Crowne Plaza Hotel Helsinki, serving as the pre-conference day of DITA Europe. Attendees can register online, and those interested in presenting can submit proposals until December 1, 2026.
