# The Mythical Man-Month in the Age of AI

Source: https://j11g.com/the-mythical-man-month-in-the-age-of-ai

## Summary
This article revisits Fred Brooks' classic *The Mythical Man-Month* through the lens of AI agents, arguing that its core insights remain valid. The author draws a parallel between adding more human developers to a late project and deploying more AI agents — neither automatically speeds things up. Sequential tasks stay sequential, and coordination overhead shifts rather than disappears.

## Key takeaways
- The "Baby Problem" still applies: nine AI agents can't deliver a baby in one month — inherently sequential work can't be parallelized away.
- Adding more agents to a late project doesn't fix it, just as adding more people doesn't, per Brooks' original law.
- Overhead hasn't disappeared with AI; it has shifted from managing people to managing agents.
- Someone still needs to define "done," review code, catch hallucinations, and integrate the pieces — the human coordination role persists.
