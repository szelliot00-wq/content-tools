# 碁の国: Play Chess as Town Building

Source: https://gonokuni.plpl.town/

## Summary
碁の国 (Kono Kuni) is a web-based Go (not chess) game application that offers multiple play modes. It allows users to play against an AI, find online opponents, play with friends via shared URLs, watch AI vs. AI matches, and review replays. The interface is in Japanese and appears to be a full-featured Go platform accessible through a browser.

## Key takeaways
- The title describes it as "Play Chess as Town Building," but the content is actually a Go (囲碁) game application — the title appears to be a mistranslation or mislabeling.
- Four play modes are available: vs. AI, online matchmaking (with AI fallback), friend matches via shared room URL, and AI vs. AI spectating.
- A replay viewer is included, suggesting game history and review functionality.
- The app has a settings menu, indicating user-configurable options.
- The interface is entirely in Japanese, targeting Japanese-speaking users.
