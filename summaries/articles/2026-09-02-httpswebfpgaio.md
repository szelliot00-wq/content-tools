# WebFPGA

Source: https://webfpga.io/

## Summary
WebFPGA is a hardware and software platform centered around an FPGA development board that can be programmed directly through a web-based IDE. The site serves as a hub linking to its Kickstarter campaign, documentation, support forum, blog, and command-line tooling for Linux users. It also showcases a standalone WebFPGA board product and community demos.

## Key takeaways
- WebFPGA offers a browser-based IDE, lowering the barrier to FPGA development without requiring local toolchain installation.
- A standalone WebFPGA board is available as a purchasable product.
- Command-line and Linux workflows are supported via IceStorm or Cloud Synthesis, with udev rules provided for device access.
- Community demos (e.g., LED Binary Counter) are shared to help users get started.
- Support is available through a dedicated forum and email (support@webfpga.io).
