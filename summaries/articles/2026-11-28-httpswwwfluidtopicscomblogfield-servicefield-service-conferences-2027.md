# 14 Must-Attend Field Service Conferences in 2027

Source: https://www.fluidtopics.com/blog/field-service/field-service-conferences-2027/

## Summary
The article from Fluid Topics lists 14 field service conferences scheduled across North America and Europe in 2027, covering events from March through late fall. It provides dates, locations, organizers, and key themes for each event to help service professionals plan their attendance. The piece is authored by Marine Vienney and published November 27, 2026.

## Key takeaways
- **14 conferences span two continents**: Events run from March (SAP for Asset & FSM Americas in San Antonio) through November (Field Service Management Summit in Berlin), with locations across the US, Germany, Netherlands, and UK.
- **Major organizers** include WBR (Service Next series), the Service Council (Smarter Services Symposium), Copperberg (Field Service Forum), TSIA (Technology & Services World), and TAC Insights (SAP-focused events).
- **Common themes across events**: service transformation, AI and technology adoption, technician enablement, workforce development, operational efficiency, and aftermarket revenue growth.
- **Technician knowledge gaps are a rising concern**: As equipment complexity increases and experienced technicians retire, equipping all technicians with expert-level knowledge is a cross-industry priority.
- **Many events have been rebranded**: Several conferences were renamed (e.g., Field Service West → Service Next West, Field Service Europe → Service Next Europe), so attendees should check for familiar events under new names.
- **European events cluster in spring and fall**: April–May (Munich, Rotterdam, Düsseldorf) and October–November (Amsterdam, Essen, Berlin) are the busiest periods for European attendees.
