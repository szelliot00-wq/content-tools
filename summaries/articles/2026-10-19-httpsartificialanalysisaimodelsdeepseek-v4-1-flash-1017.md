# DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (Max)

Source: https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/deepseek-v4-1-flash-1017

## Summary
DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (Max) is an open-weights reasoning model released in October 2026, analyzed by Artificial Analysis for intelligence, speed, cost, and verbosity. It ranks #2 out of 121 models on the Intelligence Index with a score of 45, and is notably the fastest model in its class at 230 output tokens per second. It offers competitive pricing relative to other open-weight models of similar capability, though it is significantly more verbose than peers.

## Key takeaways
- **Top-tier intelligence**: Scores 45 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, ranking #2/121 — well above the median of 19 among comparable models.
- **Fastest in class**: Outputs 230 tokens/second, ranking #6/79 overall and leading all models in its immediate peer group (next closest is Muse Wonder at 176 tok/s).
- **Competitive pricing**: $0.30/M input tokens and $1.20/M output tokens, both below median; costs $0.26 per task — second cheapest among top-tier models after MiMo-V2.6-Pro ($0.13).
- **Very verbose**: Generates 210M tokens on the Intelligence Index evaluation vs. a median of 140M, ranking #39/47 on verbosity (more tokens = higher cost and latency).
- **Large MoE architecture**: 552B total parameters, only 16B active per token — a sparse mixture-of-experts design enabling high capability at efficient inference cost.
- **Permissive open-weights**: Released under MIT license with weights on Hugging Face, supporting text + image input with a 1M token context window.
- **Reasoning model**: This is the reasoning (Max) variant; a non-reasoning version may also exist.
