# Threat of AI share crash puts world financial stability at risk, says IMF

Source: https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/oct/13/ai-share-crash-risk-global-imf-debt-corrowing-cyber-attacks

## Summary
The IMF's October 2026 Global Financial Stability Report warns that a potential AI stock market crash represents a major threat to global financial stability. Sky-high valuations, rising corporate debt, and interconnected financing arrangements among AI companies have created elevated systemic risk. An AI correction could trigger broad market downturns, with Asian markets most exposed and global spillovers likely.

## Key takeaways
- The IMF identified an AI share price crash as one of three "salient risks" to global financial stability, alongside rising government borrowing costs and a major cyber-attack.
- The "Magnificent Seven" tech stocks (Alphabet, Amazon, Apple, Meta, Microsoft, Nvidia, Tesla) now account for over 35% of the S&P 500, up from 12% a decade ago, with market cap rising 50%+ in two years.
- Global exposure to US AI assets is at an all-time high, meaning a correction would have broad international consequences — Asia most exposed, UK and eurozone somewhat insulated.
- The IMF flagged growing leverage as an amplifier: corporate debt, hedge fund exposure, and retail investors borrowing to speculate in AI stocks could all trigger forced selling in a downturn.
- $300bn in transactions between hyperscalers and weaker AI firms, plus $650bn in debt tied to $1.4tn in future commitments, represents a web of "circular financing" that could accelerate any crash.
- The IMF expressed doubt that AI's economic gains will justify the massive infrastructure investment underway, suggesting current valuations may not be sustainable.
