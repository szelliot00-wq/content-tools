# EU Machinery Regulation 2027: What Documentation Teams Need to Know

Source: https://www.fluidtopics.com/blog/content-ops/eu-machinery-regulation-what-documentation-teams-need/

## Summary
Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 replaces the existing Machinery Directive and takes effect on January 20, 2027. The article, published by Fluid Topics, outlines what technical writers and documentation teams need to prepare for under the new regulation, focusing on changes to instructions for use and technical documentation requirements.

## Key takeaways
- **Regulatory shift**: The EU Machinery Directive is being replaced by Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, with a compliance deadline of January 20, 2027.
- **Digital-first delivery**: The new regulation emphasizes digital delivery of instructions for use, moving away from paper-only requirements.
- **Foreseeable misuse**: Documentation must now explicitly address foreseeable misuse scenarios, expanding the scope of what technical writers must cover.
- **Translation requirements**: There are updated obligations around translating documentation for each member state market.
- **Change control**: Teams need robust processes for managing and tracking changes to technical documentation to stay compliant.
