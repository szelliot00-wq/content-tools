# TiVo to charge money for skipping commercials in your own recordings

Source: https://cordcuttersnews.com/tivo-plans-to-end-free-automatic-commercial-skipping-in-november-tests-paid-premium-replacement-service/

## Summary
TiVo is ending its free SkipMode commercial-skipping feature on November 2, 2026, replacing it with a paid add-on called Premium Auto Commercial Skip. The one-button and automatic ad-skipping functionality will move behind a paywall, though manual skipping via the 30-second skip button and fast-forward will remain free. This change reflects TiVo's parent company Xperi shifting its business focus away from DVR hardware toward smart-TV software and advertising technology.

## Key takeaways
- Free SkipMode ends November 2, 2026 — the one-button and automatic commercial skip features will no longer be included in the standard TiVo service.
- A paid replacement called "Premium Auto Commercial Skip" will be offered, with a 30-day free trial starting in November; pricing has not yet been announced.
- Manual commercial skipping (30-second skip button and fast-forward) remains free and is unaffected by this change.
- Existing recordings with SkipMode markers will continue to work until the cutoff date; new recordings after that date won't receive free skip data.
- No action is required before November 2 — TiVo says it will send more details before the deadline.
- The shift signals TiVo's broader retreat from the DVR market under Xperi, which has pivoted to smart-TV OS and ad technology.
- Long-time TiVo users, who often cited SkipMode as a primary reason to keep their subscriptions, now face a decision about whether the convenience is worth an extra monthly fee.
