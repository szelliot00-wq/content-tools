# Global Microsoft Outage

Source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/12/business/live-news/microsoft-global-outage-intl-hnk

## Summary
A major Microsoft outage lasting over 12 hours left millions worldwide unable to access Outlook email, Teams, and other Microsoft 365 services. The disruption spread to 74 countries and territories, affecting banks, governments, courts, airlines, and city services. Microsoft reported only "marginal success" in resolving the issue and confirmed the outage was not caused by a cyberattack.

## Key takeaways
- The outage affected Outlook, Teams, and other Microsoft 365 tools, with impacts confirmed in 74 countries within about an hour of onset.
- Banks (Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs in Hong Kong), city governments across the US, airlines, and courts were among those disrupted.
- Microsoft said the outage was not a cybersecurity breach but had not fully resolved it after 12+ hours, citing only "marginal success" in patching servers.
- US city governments (Miami Beach, Austin, Jacksonville, Baltimore County, and others) urged residents to contact agencies by phone while email remained down; emergency services stayed operational.
- Banks warned customers to watch for scammers looking to exploit the confusion around the outage.
- An EV manufacturer postponed its earnings call due to the disruption.
- The outage prompted renewed discussion in Europe about over-reliance on US technology infrastructure.
