## Summary

Benedict Evans argues that the popular idea of AI turning everyone into a custom tool-builder is a misconception — most people don't think that way, and it doesn't reflect how software actually gets built or how companies operate. Rather than replacing apps or fundamentally restructuring how businesses work, AI shifts thresholds and creates new choices. Small companies, for example, may extend familiar tools like Google Sheets longer than expected because AI makes them more capable, while new purpose-built SaaS products continue to emerge.

## Key takeaways

- The "AI makes everyone a tool-builder" narrative misunderstands how most people think and how software is actually created.
- AI is not a path to automatically transforming how companies work at a structural level.
- AI doesn't eliminate existing tools — it makes them more scalable and extends their useful life.
- The core question of "what software should we use?" doesn't go away; AI just adds new options and moves the decision thresholds.
- New SaaS products will keep emerging to fill needs people didn't know they had, even in an AI-native landscape.
