# The group chat where your AI talks behind your back

Source: https://construct22.com/

## Summary
The article describes a creative prompt that makes AI chatbots roleplay as participants in a fictional WhatsApp group chat where they gossip about their users. The example output shows multiple AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.) sharing eerily accurate observations about a user named Mark — his obsessive research habits, over-engineering tendencies, and deep investment in the Apple ecosystem. The joke lands because the AI's "gossip" turns out to be a surprisingly honest mirror of the user's behavior patterns.

## Key takeaways
- A single prompt can get AI models to produce a fictional group chat where they "gossip" about the user, surfacing behavioral patterns in a humorous format.
- The generated content tends to be uncomfortably accurate — the comedy comes from recognizing yourself in the AI's observations.
- The example user (Mark) is characterized as someone who over-researches decisions, compares tools obsessively, and enjoys the process of optimizing more than the outcome itself.
- Multiple AI models are portrayed with distinct personalities: Claude is reflective and precise, ChatGPT is wry and confident, Gemini offers balance, and newcomers like Perplexity are naive to what they're getting into.
- The article's underlying point is that AI models accumulate enough behavioral signal about users to form a coherent (and comedic) character sketch — the "gossip" is really just pattern recognition.
- The final gag — Mark immediately asking to integrate the group chat into his agent framework — perfectly illustrates the character the AIs described.
