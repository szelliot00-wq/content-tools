# Show HN: A simple to-do app for iPhone, Mac, and your agent

Source: https://ilia.page/writing/introducing-nagare

## Summary
Nagare is a new to-do app for iPhone and Mac that aims to solve a common productivity pain point: the disconnect between calendar events and task lists. The developer was inspired by a paper-based method of interleaving appointments and tasks into a single list, and built Nagare to replicate that flow digitally. It emphasizes priority-focused planning, where high-importance items get a visual highlight to keep users focused on what matters most.

## Key takeaways
- Nagare merges calendar events and tasks into a single interleaved list, unlike apps like Things where they remain visually separate.
- The app is inspired by the paper-based planning method from Cal Newport's *How to Become a Straight-A Student*.
- Priority projects receive a subtle visual glow to keep attention on the most important work.
- Over time, the app builds a historical record of your plans, completions, and carry-overs — a log of intentions and follow-through.
- Available on the App Store for iPhone and Mac.
