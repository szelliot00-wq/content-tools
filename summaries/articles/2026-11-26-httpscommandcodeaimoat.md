# Moat: Your OpenRouter Replacement

Source: https://commandcode.ai/moat

## Summary
Moat is a $5/month membership service from Command Code that positions itself as a wholesale AI model router — an alternative to OpenRouter. Members get access to 105+ AI models at discounted prices (up to 70% off), with no usage limits (no 5-hour or weekly caps) and zero router fees, unlike competitors who add 5–8% on top. The pitch is that the membership pays for itself quickly once monthly AI spend exceeds a relatively low threshold.

## Key takeaways
- **$5/month membership** unlocks wholesale pricing on 105+ AI models from providers like DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Z.ai, and inclusionAI.
- **Discounts range from 30–70%** off standard list prices depending on the model.
- **No usage limits** — no 5-hour windows, no weekly caps, unlike competitors such as the "GOAT" plan ($10/mo) which imposes both.
- **Zero router fee** — other routers (including OpenRouter) charge 5–8% on top of model prices; Moat does not.
- **Compared to GOAT ($10/mo):** GOAT gives $100/month in usage then list price with limits; Moat gives unlimited usage at discounted wholesale prices for half the subscription cost.
- **ROI calculator included** — the site lets you model savings by monthly spend, team size, and model tier to see when the $5/mo membership breaks even (apparently quickly at modest spend levels).
