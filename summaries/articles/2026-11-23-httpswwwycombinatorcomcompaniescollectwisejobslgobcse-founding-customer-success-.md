# CollectWise (YC F24) Is Hiring

Source: https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/collectwise/jobs/lgobCse-founding-customer-success-manager

## Summary
CollectWise, a Y Combinator F24-backed startup, is hiring a Founding Customer Success Manager in New York City. The company uses generative AI to automate consumer debt collection, claiming their AI agents already outperform human collectors by 2x at lower cost. They've grown to $3M ARR in just over a year with a 22-person team and are targeting $10M+ ARR.

## Key takeaways
- **Role:** Founding CSM responsible for customer relationships, revenue expansion, and AI agent optimization — a hybrid of customer success, account management, and operations
- **Compensation:** $160K–$210K OTE ($130K–$150K base + variable), plus 0.05%–0.10% equity
- **Requirements:** 3+ years in customer-facing roles, strong analytical and communication skills, commercial mindset, and comfort working cross-functionally with engineering
- **Company traction:** $3M annualized revenue in ~1 year; AI agents outperforming human debt collectors 2x at lower cost
- **Market:** Targeting the $35B US consumer debt collection market with autonomous AI agents and an integrated legal network
- **Stage:** Early-stage (founded 2024), well-funded, 22-person team — this is a founding-level hire with direct access to the CEO
