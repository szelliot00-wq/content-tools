# Monkey Island: all 13 versions side by side

Source: https://monkeyisland.pages.dev/

## Summary
This article is an interactive web tool that displays all 13 known versions of the classic 1990 adventure game *The Secret of Monkey Island* side by side. It allows users to browse scenes from the game and compare how each version rendered them across different platforms and hardware configurations, from early PC releases through console ports in the early 1990s.

## Key takeaways
- The game shipped across a wide range of platforms: PC (floppy and CD-ROM), Amiga, Atari ST, Macintosh, FM Towns, and Sega CD, spanning 1990–1993.
- The original PC 16-color EGA version (October 1990) is treated as the baseline artwork from which other versions derived their visuals.
- Color depth and resolution varied significantly by platform — from monochrome Hercules and 4-color CGA up to 256-color VGA and FM Towns editions.
- The tool supports side-by-side comparison of any two versions, zoom levels (1x, 2x, 4x), and a CRT filter to simulate period-accurate monitor output.
- At least 13 distinct version/hardware combinations exist, counting separate EGA and VGA modes on the same disc as separate entries.
