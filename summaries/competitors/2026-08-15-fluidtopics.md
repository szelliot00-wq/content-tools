- **Enterprise/security section removed**: "Meet the highest industry standards" content covering Security Controls, 99.9%+ uptime SLA, Compliance & Governance, and Scalability is no longer displayed on the homepage.
- **Primary CTA removed**: The "Take the Next Step" section with a "Get in touch" button is gone from the homepage body.

The product lineup in the footer nav is unchanged (Agentic AI still listed as NEW), suggesting this is a homepage redesign/simplification rather than a product change.
