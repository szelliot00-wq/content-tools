- **FAQ section expanded**: Now includes 6 specific questions, with the first one expanded by default — notably includes questions about agentic AI and MCP.
- **Customer spotlight added**: Johnson Controls quote and case study featured prominently.
- **New resource in footer**: "CIO Playbook for AI in 2026" (labeled NEW) added to top resources.
- **"Agentic AI" added to product nav** (labeled NEW) alongside existing product links.
