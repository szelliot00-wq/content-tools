# Tridion — Changes 2026-11-12

### Sites editions

## What Changed
The Tridion Sites pricing/feature comparison table was restructured — the "Essential" tier now has its own distinct column with separate feature values, rather than sharing data with adjacent tiers.

## Notable Updates
- **New pricing tier column**: The comparison table now shows three separate "Contact" pricing cells (one per tier: Essential, Team, Enterprise), suggesting the Essential tier's pricing/CTA was previously missing or merged.
- **Essential tier feature values now distinct**: Essential tier previously appeared to share cell data with Team; it now shows its own values (e.g., "# Users: Included = 10, Max = 25" appears twice — once for Essential, once for Team — confirming they're separate columns now properly populated).
- **Video player UI update**: The embedded video component was updated — the broken/disabled video element ("Unable to play media") was replaced with a functional video element, and the play button control was restructured.
- **"Additional message" form field**: The contact form's "Additional message" textarea was reordered or re-registered (ref shift), consistent with a minor form layout adjustment — no fields were added or removed.
