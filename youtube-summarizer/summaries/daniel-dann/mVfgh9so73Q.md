# Collaborator Review (2026) - Is This the Best Free Backlink Checker I’ve Tested?

## Summary
This video is a sponsored walkthrough of Collaborator, a platform that combines a free backlink checker with a sponsored content marketplace. Host Daniel demonstrates how to use the tool to analyze a competitor's backlinks, identify websites that also accept sponsored placements, and prepare a publishing order — all within a single platform. The core argument is that most backlink tools stop at showing you link sources, while Collaborator bridges the gap between research and outreach by surfacing placement opportunities directly from competitor data. It is most relevant to SEO professionals, content marketers, and digital advertisers running link-building or sponsored content campaigns.

## Key insights
- Collaborator's catalog includes over 43,000 websites and media outlets across different countries and niches that accept sponsored articles.
- The backlink checker is powered by SE Ranking; no account allows 5 checks per day, while a free account raises the limit to 20.
- Each backlink report shows up to 100 referring domains, split into "strongest" and "newest" categories, giving context on both established and recent sources.
- The key differentiator is that websites appearing in the backlink report are flagged if they are also listed in the Collaborator marketplace, enabling a direct jump from research to placement without re-searching elsewhere.
- Full website profiles inside the marketplace include publication terms, traffic history, available order options, and reviews from previous advertisers — no need to contact publishers separately.
- Users can create a project to organize campaigns, save target pages, and pre-fill instructions, which streamlines the ordering process for multiple placements.
- Filters for topic, region, and budget are available to narrow the marketplace catalog before reviewing individual options.
- When placing an order, advertisers can either upload their own article or commission the publisher to write one, and specify the target URL they want promoted.
- Daniel emphasizes that strong SEO metrics alone are not sufficient — content relevance and audience fit still require manual review before committing to a placement.

## Use cases
- **Competitor link-building research**: Finding where competitors earn backlinks and identifying which of those sources accept paid placements.
- **Sponsored content campaigns**: Advertisers looking to place articles on relevant third-party sites without cold-outreach prospecting.
- **Niche or regional targeting**: Campaigns that need to filter publishers by topic category or geographic market.
- **Budget-constrained campaigns**: Marketers who want to set a price ceiling and see viable options before reviewing individual placements.
- **Organized multi-placement campaigns**: Teams managing multiple target URLs or clients who benefit from project-level organization inside a single tool.
- **Freelance SEOs and agencies**: Professionals who need to move quickly from analysis to deliverable without juggling multiple tools.

## Patterns & frameworks
**Research-to-Placement Pipeline**
A two-stage workflow where backlink analysis directly feeds into a placement marketplace. Stage 1: run a competitor domain through the free checker to surface referring domains. Stage 2: check which of those domains are listed in the marketplace and open their profiles for vetting. This eliminates the manual step of separately hunting for contact information or guest post opportunities.

**Strongest + Newest Split**
The report separates backlinks into "strongest" (high-authority, established sources) and "newest" (recent acquisitions). This lets the user benchmark both long-term link equity and emerging link patterns from a single report.

**Metrics-Plus-Relevance Vetting**
Daniel's personal framework for evaluating a placement opportunity: quantitative check (SEO metrics, traffic history, price) followed by a qualitative check (visit the live site, read recent articles, assess audience fit). The point is that numbers are necessary but not sufficient — relevance determines whether a placement will actually perform.

**Project-Based Campaign Organization**
Before browsing the marketplace, Daniel creates a project that stores the target domain, target pages, and campaign instructions. All subsequent orders are linked to this project, keeping research, decisions, and placements in one organized place rather than scattered across browser tabs or spreadsheets.
