# Exploring Johns Hopkins University’s AI Programs from Great Learning (2026)

Video ID: `9tCny5dwGZM`

## Summary
This video reviews four AI certificate programs offered by Great Learning in collaboration with Johns Hopkins University's Whiting School of Engineering. The presenter, Daniel, walks through each program's duration, target audience, curriculum focus, and credentials awarded. The programs span a wide range of learner profiles — from developers building production AI systems to business leaders and non-technical professionals. The core argument is that these programs are differentiated by learning goal and technical background, so choosing the right one depends on your role and what you want to do with AI.

## Key insights
- **Four distinct programs** are covered, each targeting a different learner profile and career goal.
- **Certificate Program in Agentic AI** (18 weeks, 13 CEUs): Designed for developers building production-ready AI agents and multi-agent systems. Includes a Python pre-work module, then advances into agent planning, multi-agent coordination, and deployment logic. Emphasizes evaluating outputs and managing agent behavior in professional environments.
- **Certificate Program in AI Business Strategy** (10 weeks, 6 CEUs): Aimed at project managers, technology leaders, and consultants. Focuses on identifying where AI creates business value, planning adoption, and managing organizational change — not on technical implementation.
- **Applied Generative and Agentic AI** (16 weeks, 11 CEUs): For technology professionals wanting hands-on experience building AI applications. Goes beyond prompt writing to building reliable, production-grade workflows. Covers generative and agentic systems connected to real product-style use cases.
- **No-Code Generative and Agentic AI** (12 weeks, 9 CEUs): Designed for non-programmers who want to build AI workflows and business automation without writing code. Uses modern no-code platforms; includes projects like a RAG-powered document assistant and an autonomous multi-agent sales intelligence system.
- All four programs combine **self-paced study with live mentored sessions**, hands-on projects, and real-world case studies.
- Time commitment is approximately **6–10 hours per week**, designed to be manageable alongside full-time work.
- All programs award a **Johns Hopkins University certificate of completion** plus continuing education units (CEUs).
- Great Learning is described as a global edtech platform that partners with top universities and continuously updates its curriculum to reflect industry needs.
- The presenter notes he personally uses the service, framing the video as a genuine review rather than purely promotional content.

## Use cases
- **Software engineers or ML practitioners** who want to build and deploy multi-agent AI systems in production environments (Agentic AI program).
- **Product managers, CTOs, or business consultants** who need to evaluate AI opportunities, justify investment, and lead adoption initiatives without deep technical skills (AI Business Strategy program).
- **Mid-level tech professionals** (e.g., data analysts, backend developers) who want to move into applied AI development and build generative/agentic workflows (Applied Generative and Agentic AI program).
- **Non-technical professionals** — operations managers, marketers, HR leaders — who want to automate workflows and use AI tools without learning to code (No-Code program).
- Professionals exploring an **AI career pivot** who want a structured, credential-backed path rather than self-directed learning.
- Anyone needing **continuing education units** for professional certification maintenance alongside practical AI upskilling.

## Patterns & frameworks

**Learner Segmentation by Technical Background and Goal**
Great Learning's four-program structure implicitly uses a 2x2 model: axis one is technical depth (technical builder vs. business strategist), axis two is coding requirement (code vs. no-code). This framework helps learners self-select the right program rather than defaulting to the most advanced or most popular option.

**Blended Learning Model**
All programs follow a consistent structure: self-paced foundational content → live expert-led sessions → mentorship → hands-on projects → certificate. This progression moves learners from theory to applied skill in a repeatable sequence designed for working professionals.

**Foundations-to-Production Progression**
The technical programs (especially Applied Generative and Agentic AI) explicitly structure curriculum from foundational AI concepts up through real-world deployment — a "crawl, walk, run" ramp that mirrors how engineering teams actually adopt new technology stacks.

**No-Code as an Entry Point, Not a Limitation**
The No-Code program frames the absence of programming as a feature, not a constraint — allowing business-side professionals to design, test, and manage AI workflows using the same underlying concepts (RAG, multi-agent systems) as the technical programs, just via different tooling.
