## Patterns & frameworks
**One workspace for global finance** — The central framework: instead of using separate tools for international accounts, FX, transfers, cards, and expense management, consolidate everything into a single dashboard. The benefit is visibility (see what's coming in, going out, and pending) and reduced context-switching for finance teams.

**Hold-then-convert** — Rather than automatically converting incoming foreign currency, the platform advocates holding multi-currency balances and converting at interbank rates when timing is favorable. This treats FX as a decision point rather than a fixed cost.

**Local rails over SWIFT** — A repeatable pattern for international transfers: route payments through local payment infrastructure in the destination country rather than the legacy SWIFT network. This produces faster settlement (same-day or instant) at lower cost.

**Spend cycle consolidation** — Grouping the full expense lifecycle — card issuance, receipt capture, reimbursement claims, invoice management, purchase approvals, and vendor payments — into one section so finance teams can follow a spend item from request to payment without losing context across tools.

**AI-assisted compliance** — Using an automated policy agent to check every expense claim against policy rules before human review, shifting the finance team's role from manual checking to exception handling. Flags duplicates and violations proactively rather than reactively.
