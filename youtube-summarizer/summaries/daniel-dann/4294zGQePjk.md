## Key insights
- **Virtual numbers in 100+ countries**: Numbers can be claimed directly from the dashboard; some are included at no extra cost depending on the subscription plan.
- **Call routing strategies**: Three inbound routing options — simultaneous ringing (all agents ring at once), sequential ringing (agents contacted one by one), and round robin (calls distributed evenly across agents).
- **AI Receptionist**: An AI voice agent that answers calls outside business hours using business-provided information (hours, services, FAQs, pricing). It can escalate to a human when needed. Response quality depends entirely on what information is configured.
- **Power Dialer**: Automates outbound calling campaigns by working through a contact list sequentially, eliminating manual dialing. Supports call dispositions (outcomes), import of contacts, and configurable prep/post-call time. Only available on the premium plan.
- **Supervisor monitoring tools**: Three modes — *Listen* (silent monitoring), *Whisper* (coach the agent privately without the customer hearing), and *Barge In* (join the call audibly to speak with both parties).
- **AI conversation analytics**: Post-call tools include transcription, sentiment analysis, call summaries, reason-for-contact identification, and frequently used word tracking. These help managers spot trends (e.g., many customers asking the same question) without listening to every recording.
- **SMS/MMS support**: Messaging capability depends on the specific phone number claimed and the destination country — businesses should verify this before relying on it.
- **Unified inbox**: Keeps calls, SMS, and MMS in one place so agents don't jump between tools.
- **Saved replies and scheduled messages**: Canned responses for recurring questions and time-scheduled follow-up messages support consistent, efficient agent communication.
- **CRM integrations**: Connects with HubSpot and Salesforce; users should verify exactly what data syncs before building workflows around it.
- **Calilio Hub (prepaid packages)**: Prepaid minute bundles for specific destinations, offering up to 30% savings vs. pay-as-you-go. Packages have a validity period and unused minutes expire — suited for seasonal spikes or short-term campaigns.
- **Black Friday promotion**: 50% cashback credited instantly to the Calilio wallet when purchasing any phone number.

## Patterns & frameworks

**Cloud Phone System Model**
Replaces physical PBX hardware with an internet-based interface. Enables location-independent calling, shared numbers, and centralized management through a dashboard — the foundational architecture the entire platform is built on.

**Inbound Call Routing Strategies**
A three-mode framework for distributing incoming calls: *Simultaneous* (maximum speed, first available answers), *Sequential* (priority-ordered agents), *Round Robin* (even workload distribution). Choosing the right mode depends on team size and call volume patterns.

**AI Receptionist Setup Process**
A repeatable configuration pattern: (1) provide business knowledge (hours, FAQs, pricing), (2) write agent instructions, (3) select a voice, (4) test responses, (5) assign to a number. Functions as an always-on first line of response with human escalation as a fallback.

**Supervisor Oversight Ladder**
An escalating intervention model — Listen → Whisper → Barge In — giving supervisors graduated levels of involvement during a live call, from passive observation to full participation.

**Post-Call AI Review Loop**
A pattern for continuous improvement: transcription converts calls to text → sentiment analysis flags tone → summaries surface key points → topic/reason tagging reveals trends → managers investigate recurring issues and adjust processes.

**Prepaid Usage Packaging (Hub)**
A cost management pattern for variable demand: estimate usage volume by period → purchase a matched prepaid bundle at a discount → consume within validity window → avoid over-provisioning a permanent plan. Best applied to predictable short-term spikes.
