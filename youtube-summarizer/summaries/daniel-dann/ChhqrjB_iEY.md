## Key insights
- Yinso AI generates full songs (lyrics, vocals, melody) from a text description — no music theory or production skills required
- The platform has two modes: **Simple** (quick, minimal control) and **Advanced** (control over lyrics, style, vocal direction)
- Each generation produces **two versions** from the same prompt, allowing side-by-side comparison of melody and vocal interpretation
- Generic prompts ("make me a sad friendship song") produce generic results; **specific story details** produce more personal, emotionally accurate output
- The **"Write with AI"** feature lets you feed additional specific memory details to expand and refine lyrics before regenerating — Daniel used a concrete moment (walking along the Seine at night) to get more vivid lyrical imagery
- You can bring AI-expanded lyrics back into the creation area and generate a new version with that improved material
- Vocal style can be left open for Yinso to decide, or specified — Daniel chose **combined female and male lead vocals** for added emotional depth
- The finished product is shareable directly — Daniel sent the track to his friend, who was "literally blown away"

## Patterns & frameworks

**The "Story-First" prompting approach**
Rather than starting with a genre or mood, start with a specific real memory or event. Describe what actually happened, the setting, the sensory details, and the emotional arc. The more concrete the input, the more personal the output. Generic emotion → generic song; specific story → specific song.

**The four-step Yinso workflow**
1. Tell Yinso the story (what happened, specific details)
2. Choose the sound (genre, mood, vocal style)
3. Refine lyrics using the "Write with AI" feature with additional memory details
4. Compare the two generated versions and select the keeper

**Iterative refinement loop**
Generate → identify what's missing → feed a more specific detail into "Write with AI" → expand the lyrics → regenerate with the improved material. This loop moves the song from generically emotional to specifically personal.
