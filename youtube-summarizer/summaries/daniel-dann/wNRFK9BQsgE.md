- Installation is handled through the **HolaFly mobile app** — no manual entry of activation codes; the app walks through each step and the eSIM is given a custom label for easy switching
- The phone connects to **5G automatically** when available at the destination, with no manual network selection required
- The presenter noted that after setup, he "stopped thinking about the internet altogether" — the key UX win is invisibility of the connection

- The presenter only discovered Always On *after* it saved him when he unexpectedly extended his trip by a few days — framing it as a genuine safety net, not a marketing add-on

## Patterns & frameworks
**Install-before-you-leave model**
Buy and configure the eSIM at home on a stable connection; arrival at the destination triggers automatic activation. This flips the traditional "find a SIM shop after landing" pattern into a pre-departure setup, reducing arrival-day friction to zero.

**Unlimited + Always On layered data model**
HolaFly structures connectivity in two tiers: (1) an unlimited primary plan scoped to the trip duration and destination, and (2) an automatic 1 GB/month global backup ("Always On") that kicks in when the primary plan expires or when the traveler is outside the primary plan's coverage window. The backup is not marketed as a selling point upfront — it exists as a passive safety net that activates silently.

**Gamified loyalty loop (HolaFly Coins)**
Each purchase earns coins that raise an account level. Higher levels unlock larger discounts and additional travel perks. This is a standard retention mechanic designed to convert one-time buyers into repeat customers, particularly relevant for frequent travelers who would otherwise comparison-shop each trip.

**Three-step purchase funnel**
HolaFly's own framing on their website: (1) select destination and duration, (2) install eSIM via app, (3) activate on arrival. The simplicity of this framing is a product positioning choice — reducing perceived complexity for a customer segment (travelers) that values low cognitive load.
