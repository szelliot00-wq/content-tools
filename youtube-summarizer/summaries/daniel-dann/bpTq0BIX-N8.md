## Patterns & frameworks

**1. Reference-First Generation**
Before writing a prompt, upload visual references that define key subjects (character, prop, environment). This grounds the model in concrete visuals and reduces randomness, improving consistency from the first generation.

**2. Minimal-Direction Prompting**
Keep prompts simple and scene-level rather than specifying every camera move or moment. This leaves room for the model to generate natural motion rather than producing stiff, over-specified output.

**3. Clip-as-Continuation Reference**
Use the last generated clip as the visual input for the next generation, rather than re-prompting from scratch. This is the core technique for maintaining character and environment consistency across a multi-clip sequence — each clip "inherits" from the prior one.

**4. Generate → Compose → Sequence Pipeline**
A three-stage repeatable workflow: (1) generate clips in Canvas using references and prompts, (2) iterate using clip-chaining for continuation, (3) assemble in Compose on the timeline. The goal is to complete the full arc inside one platform without exporting between tools.
