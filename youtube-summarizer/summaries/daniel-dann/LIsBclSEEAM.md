## Summary
This is a sponsored review of Fetra AI (referred to throughout as "Petra AI"), an AI-powered social media marketing platform. Host Daniel walks through the platform using a fictional brand called "PetPaws/Petclaw" to demonstrate how it ingests brand data, generates viral-style video content, and manages a social publishing workflow. The core argument is that Fetra AI reduces the time cost of social content production by automating trend research, video adaptation, and posting schedules — while still requiring human review before publishing. It is most relevant to small brands and lean marketing teams that need to produce social content consistently but lack the resources for a dedicated creative team.

## Key insights
- **Business DNA setup**: Fetra scans a brand's website and automatically extracts its logo, colors, typography, and a short brand overview to form a "Business DNA" profile — this profile informs all subsequent content generation.
- **Blitz feature**: Acts as a recommendation feed of popular social video formats, automatically adapting each trending format's hook and structure to fit your brand. Users can reject, edit, or approve each suggestion, and control how often the brand appears and which creative formats are surfaced.
- **Templates feature**: Lets users manually select a video structure (e.g., green screen), pick a reference, and have Fetra generate a brand-adapted version — more user-directed than Blitz.
- **Viral Remix feature**: Users supply their own short reference clip; the platform breaks it into shots, explains each segment, then rewrites the script around the brand before rendering. The reviewer emphasizes checking the storyboard before final render to avoid wasting credits on a result that follows the reference too literally.
- **Credit system**: Renders consume platform credits, so the workflow encourages reviewing storyboards and scripts before committing to final video generation.
- **Marco (Social Growth Manager)**: An AI agent that produces a broader content strategy — first generating a positioning/strategy document for approval, then creating a full week of post drafts (captions + visuals) that follow that positioning consistently.
- **Publishing and scheduling**: Fetra connects to social accounts (Pinterest demonstrated), allows scheduling posts to specific dates/times, and surfaces a calendar view to confirm scheduling — making the tool functional end-to-end, not just a content generator.
- **Library and calendar**: Approved assets from Blitz and Viral Remix are stored in a library; the calendar supports forward planning of the content schedule.
- **AI Influencer section**: Users can create a synthetic AI persona by describing appearance details; Fetra generates a base portrait that can be used to produce consistent images or videos featuring that character.
- **Sponsorship disclosure**: The video is explicitly sponsored by Fetra AI, and the host offers a 20% discount code in the description — relevant context for evaluating objectivity.
- **Reviewer's honest caveats**: Despite the sponsorship, the reviewer repeatedly notes that every caption and visual should be checked before publishing, and that the platform is a starting point, not a replacement for final creative judgment.

## Patterns & frameworks

**Business DNA**
A brand profile automatically generated from a website scan. It captures visual identity (logo, colors, fonts) and a written brand overview. Serves as the persistent context layer that all content generation draws from, so outputs stay on-brand without manual re-briefing each time.

**Blitz (Trend-to-Brand Adaptation Loop)**
A recommendation-feed workflow: the platform continuously surfaces trending video formats → auto-adapts each to the brand → the user reviews and approves/rejects/edits. Configurable via a "Configure" panel that controls brand appearance frequency and allowable creative formats. Designed to replace manual trend research with a curated, brand-filtered feed.

**Viral Remix (Reference-In, Brand-Out)**
A user-initiated adaptation process: supply a reference clip → platform segments it into shots and narrates each → user optionally adds brand images and regenerates the script → storyboard review → final render. The intermediate review step is the key pattern — it catches over-literal interpretations before credits are spent on rendering.

**Marco / Social Growth Manager (Strategy-First Content Planning)**
A two-phase planning pattern: (1) generate and approve a positioning/strategy document, then (2) generate a week of content drafts that are anchored to that approved strategy. This ensures that individual posts reflect a coherent brand direction rather than being generated in isolation.

**Storyboard Gate**
A cost-control pattern embedded in the Viral Remix workflow: always review the storyboard before triggering final render, since rendering consumes more credits than script generation. Prevents wasted spend on outputs that need significant rework.
