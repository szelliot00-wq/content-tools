# Can Your AI Use Sensitive Data Safely? 5 Capabilities You Need

Video ID: `jnP9AgtX0qo`

## Summary
The video outlines the tension between using sensitive data to get the best AI results and protecting that data from exposure. It argues there is a third path — meaningful AI outcomes without compliance risk — built on five foundational controls. The presenter walks through each control systematically, framing them as prerequisites for treating sensitive data as a strategic asset rather than a liability.

## Key insights
- **AI processes data at machine speed, so controls must match that speed** — legacy human-scale governance is too slow to keep up with AI data consumption.
- **The most useful data is usually the most sensitive** — customer PII, health records, and financial data are exactly what makes AI powerful, creating a direct tension between utility and risk.
- **Control 1 — Visibility:** You need a single, continuous view of where sensitive data lives and moves across on-prem, cloud, SaaS, endpoints, and networks — including who uses it and why.
- **Control 2 — Classification:** Automated, continuous classification by sensitivity level must happen *before* data reaches AI systems; manual tagging is too slow and error-prone, and reactive governance means you're already behind.
- **Control 3 — Data residency, sovereignty, and localization:** Residency = where data is stored; sovereignty = whose laws govern it; localization = legal requirements to store *and process* within a jurisdiction. Cloud environments make this particularly complex, and legal teams must be involved.
- **Control 4 — Access control (IAM):** Structured around four A's — Administration (provisioning with least privilege), Authentication (proving identity via MFA/passkeys), Authorization (role-based and conditional access), and Audit (verifying the first three were done correctly).
- **Control 5 — Evidence (Audit trail):** Automated, immutable logging of all data movements and AI workload interactions is essential — auditors and regulators need real evidence, not reconstructed records.
- **Trustworthy AI depends on trustworthy data** — the five controls together shift sensitive data from a compliance risk into a strategic asset the organization can fully leverage.
