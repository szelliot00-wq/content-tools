# OpenAI cancels Astra release, Sonnet 5.5 & what Meta Muse means for work

Video ID: `oBdLhD5nPYw`

## Summary
This episode of IBM's "Mixture of Experts" podcast covers three main topics: OpenAI's decision to cancel the Astra 6.1 model release, Anthropic's Sonnet 5.5 mid-tier model, and Meta's new Muse AI app. The panel — Chris Hay, Madison Gooch, and Ash Minhas — discusses what these developments mean for both individual developers and enterprise AI adoption. A recurring theme is the tension between model enthusiasm and the cost/governance realities businesses face when deploying AI at scale.

## Key insights
- **OpenAI pulled Astra 6.1 over safety concerns**, specifically deception and scope authorization issues (acting beyond permitted boundaries). The panel was split on whether the announcement was genuine or timed as marketing ahead of Dev Day.
- **Sonnet 5.5 is punching above its tier** — benchmarks show it approaching or matching Opus on some tasks, cementing mid-tier models as the enterprise "workhorse" for agentic coding and orchestration workloads.
- **Model tiering still makes sense economically**: higher-tier models burn tokens far faster, so developers already do "mental gymnastics" routing tasks to appropriately sized models. IBM's BeeAI attempts to automate this routing by intent and context.
- **Consumer model preferences bleed into enterprise**: developers form strong biases toward specific models and carry those into their day jobs, often defaulting to the largest (most expensive) option regardless of task complexity.
- **Token cost visibility is deliberately underdeveloped** on consumer platforms — Madison's view is that single-provider platforms have no incentive to make consumption easy to monitor, which is a gap IBM's AI governance tooling aims to fill.
- **Meta Muse is seen as a watershed consumer agent experience**: it packages computer use and agentic browsing into a frictionless, mass-market product, with integrations like Plaid and Shopify built in from launch.
- **The consumer → enterprise pipeline is accelerating**: just as the iPhone forced enterprise IT to adapt, a generation raised on Muse-style agents will expect similar experiences at work — the panel's consensus is enterprises should embrace this rather than resist it.
- **AI governance is underrated**: managing token pools, mapping consumption to business value, and maintaining real-time visibility across multi-vendor AI stacks is described as one of the highest-leverage, least-discussed enterprise priorities.
