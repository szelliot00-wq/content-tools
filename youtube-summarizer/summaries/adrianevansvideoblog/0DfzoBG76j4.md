# ClearBank made history as the UK's first new clearing bank in more than 250 years

Video ID: `0DfzoBG76j4`

## Summary
This episode of the Payments Leader Show features Mark Felis, CEO of ClearBank — the UK's first new clearing bank in over 250 years — in conversation with host Adrien Evans. Felis covers ClearBank's origins as a cloud-native, API-first infrastructure provider for fintechs, its expansion into Europe and digital assets, and his vision for the future of payments. The discussion spans the technical and strategic challenges of cross-border payments, the role of infrastructure in enabling innovation, and how ClearBank's 10-year-old tech stack gives it a structural advantage over incumbent banks.

## Key insights
- **No legacy debt is a genuine competitive advantage.** ClearBank's oldest technology is from 2016 — 20–25 years newer than incumbent banks — giving it agility, resilience, and the ability to move fast without modernization debt.
- **Infrastructure is the hidden differentiator.** Consumers judge apps by their UI, but what actually determines experience quality is the infrastructure underneath — batch processing, settlement windows, and fragmented rails are what cause 4-day international transfers.
- **Innovation and resilience are not in tension.** Being cloud-native (Azure) and building from scratch means ClearBank can ship quickly while maintaining low change failure rates — rates that have dropped further with AI-assisted deployment checks.
- **Cross-border payments remain the biggest unsolved problem.** Different rails, compliance standards, operating hours, intermediary chains, and incentives to hold float all compound into slow, expensive, opaque international transfers.
- **Stablecoins are the practical answer to cross-border friction.** ClearBank's partnership with Circle (EURC minting/burning) reflects their view that blockchain-based digital money enables near-instant, transparent, low-cost cross-border settlement — a path they see as inevitable.
- **Embedded finance will blur the line between banks and non-banks.** As financial services get embedded directly into non-financial products and customer journeys, the boundary between what constitutes a bank will become increasingly unclear.
- **Agentic payments are the next emerging category.** As AI agents act autonomously on behalf of users, a high volume of small-value automated purchases will require payments infrastructure that is real-time, programmable, and reliable.
- **Customer demand, not tech ambition, drove ClearBank's expansion.** The move into Europe and into corporate banking were both pulled by existing client demand — Felis frames all product and market decisions around unlocking client potential rather than innovation for its own sake.
- **The UK fintech ecosystem is a genuine global success story.** Felis credits the UK as a world-leading hub for fintech over the past decade, viewing it as a significant national economic asset in a growth-focused environment.
- **Staying ahead, not catching up, is the strategic posture.** Felis consistently distinguishes between reacting to change and leading it — citing ClearBank's history of firsts (first clearing bank, first cloud-native, first Netherlands digital asset license) as the standard to maintain.
