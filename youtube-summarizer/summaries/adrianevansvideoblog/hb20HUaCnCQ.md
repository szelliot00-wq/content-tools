# To build your brand - be authentic, conduct business in the right honest way and stay curious.

Video ID: `hb20HUaCnCQ`

## Summary
This episode of the Payments Leader Show features host Adrian Evans in conversation with Chloe, a fractional CMO with 16+ years of experience at L'Oreal and Diageo. They explore how brands become cultural assets rather than just products, covering storytelling, consumer obsession, trust-building, and brand consistency. The discussion draws on real-world examples from Guinness, Marks & Spencer, Hampstead Tea, and others to ground the principles in practice.

## Key insights
- **Brand equity is built like a bird's nest** — accumulated through many small, consistent touchpoints over time, not switched on overnight or by riding a single cultural trend.
- **Authenticity is the foundation of trust** — brands that do the right thing (e.g., Hampstead Tea's fair trade practices and cow gifting program) generate compelling stories naturally; marketers just need to surface them.
- **Consistency is your most underrated tool** — the only people who get bored of a brand's messaging are those inside it; consumers need repeated exposure to internalize brand meaning.
- **Consumer obsession beats competitor obsession** — brands focused on chasing competitors are always in catch-up mode and risk copying someone else's story, which is meaningless; return to "who is our consumer and what do they need?"
- **Find the enemy in the category** — transformative brands (e.g., Tony's Chocolonely, Bulk Beans) identify a systemic problem others ignore, then position themselves as the solution before consumers can even articulate the need.
- **Small trust failures matter as much as big ones** — the M&S proactive delivery delay email, which automatically refunded next-day delivery without requiring customer action, shows that seamless cross-functional thinking prevents trust erosion at the micro level.
- **Speak to at least one consumer per week** — surveys give percentages; real conversations give motivation and pain points, which are the true unlocking insight.
- **Analog and experiential marketing will swing back** — in a world of AI-generated content, brands that create real, human, joyful experiences (e.g., Botivo's yellow piano at events) will cut through.
- **Play to your strengths, not your weaknesses** — in career development, energy spent optimizing natural weaknesses yields far less return than doubling down on what you do well.
- **Deliver the promise before surprise and delight** — get the basics right across every team first; brand equity gives you forgiveness when things occasionally go wrong.
