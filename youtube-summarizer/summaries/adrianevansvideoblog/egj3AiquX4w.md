# Performance Is Not Enough: Future Proofing the Executive Career

Video ID: `egj3AiquX4w`

## Summary
Adrian Evans, founder of The Executive Advantage, joins host Justin Hana to discuss how senior executives can future-proof their careers in an increasingly volatile job market. Drawing on his background as a headhunter and a personal mission to impact 20,000 careers, Adrian explains why performance alone no longer protects executive roles. He walks through his 14-step framework, the diagnostic tool at executiveadvantage.co.uk, and practical strategies around networking, outreach, and negotiation.

## Key insights
- **Performance alone is not enough.** Political capital, reputation, visibility, and sponsorship are equally critical — executives who rely solely on results leave themselves exposed to reorganizations and redundancies.
- **Anticipate jolts before they happen.** Warning signs like being excluded from key meetings or being sidelined during M&A activity can signal an impending redundancy months in advance, giving time to prepare.
- **The three areas executives score lowest on are clarity, outreach, and negotiation** — all learnable skills that are triple-weighted in Adrian's diagnostic because they most differentiate successful career transitions.
- **The hidden job market is where most opportunities live.** The best roles go to those most connected, not most qualified — proactive relationship-building before you need it is the single biggest career investment.
- **Interviews now matter more than ever**, with cultural fit and future potential often outweighing current skill sets, especially as AI accelerates the pace at which existing skills become obsolete.
- **Negotiation should be framed around mutual self-interest.** Understanding what problem an employer is trying to solve — revenue growth, cost reduction, or risk mitigation — lets candidates frame their value and request appropriate compensation.
- **Proactivity is the standout trait when hiring.** A growth mindset and willingness to go first (demonstrated by action, not credentials) outweighs a static track record.
- **AI + human intelligence combined wins.** Using AI tools for outreach and proposition-crafting while keeping humans in the loop is the model for career success going forward.
